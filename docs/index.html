Know the boundaries

Why are some tests supposed to fail?

The starter smoke tests are green. The separate business acceptance suite describes behaviour you must repair. Its initial red state is intentional—not an installation problem. Passing smoke alone does not pass the assessment.

Is the timer secure or monitored?

No. Timing and no-AI rules are honour/proctor-based. The form saves on your device; it does not upload answers. A coordinator can observe start/end with your knowledge. No hidden surveillance is included.

Can I use AI or documentation?

No in the separate reasoning stage. Yes during practical work with disclosure. Explain and verify your work. No real API/LLM credentials are required.

What if Flutter or setup is unfamiliar?

Setup is outside the clock. Flutter is a bounded learning task, not a claimed primary skill. The module guide explains a fallback and how to label unexecuted work honestly.

FINISH WITH A HANDOVER, NOT A PROMISE.

Code. Evidence. Decisions. Next owner.

Use your own branch. The public starter does not grant upstream push access. A private ZIP handover is recommended; a public fork is optional. Keep cognitive answers, recordings, personal data and secrets out of public Git history.
